# Guide for Community Health Workers

How to use the health screening application on your phone.

**The most important thing to know:** this application works without internet. You can
open it, find patients, record a screening, see the result and make a referral with no
signal at all. Your work is saved on your phone straight away and sent to the health
facility later, by itself, when you have a connection.

---

## 1. Setting up your phone (once)

You need internet for this first step only.

1. Open the link your supervisor gave you.
2. Your phone will offer to **install** or **add to home screen**. Say yes. The
   application then opens like any other app, with its own icon.
3. Enter your **username** and **password**.
4. Choose a **PIN** of 4 to 12 digits and enter it twice.

**About your PIN**

- You will use it every day to open the application.
- It protects the patient records stored on your phone. If your phone is lost, nobody
  can read them without it.
- It is **not saved anywhere** and cannot be recovered. If you forget it, your
  supervisor will have to set the phone up again, and any records that have not yet been
  sent would be lost.
- Do not use 1234, 0000, or the same digit repeated.
- Do not share it, and do not write it on the phone.

---

## 2. Every day

Open the application and enter your PIN. **No internet is needed.**

If you enter the wrong PIN ten times the application stops accepting attempts and asks
you to contact your supervisor. Your records are still safe.

To lock the phone yourself, tap the lock icon at the top right of the home screen. Do
this whenever you put the phone down.

---

## 3. The strip at the top

A coloured strip sits under the title on every screen. It always tells you where your
work is:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ONLINE — EVERYTHING SENT** | Everything you have recorded is at the health facility |
| **ONLINE — 3 WAITING TO SEND** | You have a connection; 3 records are on their way |
| **OFFLINE — 12 RECORDS SAVED ON THIS PHONE** | No connection. 12 records are safe on your phone and will go automatically |
| **SENDING 7 OF 12 RECORDS…** | Sending right now |
| **CANNOT SEND YET — 12 SAVED SAFELY** | The server cannot be reached. Your records are safe and will be sent later |

**You never need to do anything to send records.** The application does it whenever it
finds a connection. The strip is there so you always know, not so you have to act.

---

## 4. Registering a new patient

Tap **+ Register a new patient**.

Fill in first name, last name, age, sex and village. The phone number is optional — it
is used only to send a referral reminder by text message.

### Consent

Before you save, read the consent statement on the screen to the patient:

> "I would like to record your health information so the health facility can care for
> you. It is kept private and only shared with health workers who are treating you. You
> can say no, and you can ask us to remove it later."

Then tap **Patient agreed** or **Patient declined**, whichever they said.

If the patient declines, record only what is needed to help them today and do not share
their details further.

Tap **Save patient**. The patient is saved on your phone immediately and given a short
patient code, for example `P-RFA2`. That code identifies them at the facility.

---

## 5. Screening a patient

Find the patient (tap **Patients**, or search by name, code or village), open them, and
tap **Start a new screening**. There are four steps.

### Step 1 — Vital signs

Enter what you measured: blood pressure, temperature, pulse, weight, and oxygen or blood
sugar if you have the equipment.

> **Leave blank anything you did not measure.**
> Blank is not the same as normal. The application simply skips a measurement you did
> not take. Never guess a number — a guessed reading can produce a wrong result.

Tap **Next: symptoms**.

### Step 2 — Symptoms

Tap every symptom the patient has. Tap again to remove one.

For each symptom you select, a panel appears below asking:

- **How bad is it?** — Mild, Moderate or Severe. Ask the patient; use their answer.
- **For how many days?** — how long they have had it. This matters: some rules only
  apply when a symptom has lasted several days.

There is a **Notes** box for anything else worth recording.

Tap **Check result**.

### Step 3 — The result

The result appears **immediately**. There is no waiting and no internet needed.

You will see one of three:

> ### 🔴 RED — URGENT REFERRAL
> Danger signs were found. **Refer the patient to the health facility now.** Do not wait
> for the next visit. Arrange transport if you can.

> ### 🟠 AMBER — FOLLOW-UP NEEDED
> Something needs checking, but it is not an emergency. Arrange a follow-up visit and
> share the relevant health message.

> ### 🟢 GREEN — NO DANGER SIGNS FOUND
> Nothing worrying was found today. Share a health message and tell the patient to come
> back if they feel worse.

Under the result you will see:

- **What to do** — the action to take, in plain words.
- **Why** — the indicators that caused this result, for example "Very high blood
  pressure together with a persistent headache".
- **More detail** — tap to see a fuller explanation of each rule that applied.

> **Always use your own judgement.**
> This application helps you; it does not decide. If you are worried about a patient,
> refer them — even if the result is GREEN. If the result is RED but you believe it is a
> measurement error, measure again before acting.

Tap **Continue**.

### Step 4 — Referral and follow-up

If the result was RED, **Refer to a facility** is already selected.

- **Which facility?** Type the name.
- **How urgent?** "Go now", "Within 24 hours", or "When they can". For a RED result this
  is already set to "Go now".
- **Follow-up date** — when you will check on the patient. Optional but useful: it puts
  the patient on your Follow-up list.
- **Referral note** — anything the facility should know. This goes to the facility, not
  by text message.

If the patient has a phone number, a **text message reminder** appears. It contains only
their first name, where to go, and the referral code — **no health information is ever
sent by text message**. Tap **Open in messages** to send it from your own phone.

Tap **Save screening**.

The whole encounter is saved on your phone at once. You can close the application
immediately; nothing is lost.

---

## 6. Patient history

Open any patient to see all their previous visits, newest first: the date, the blood
pressure and other readings, the symptoms recorded, the result, and any referral.

This works offline, like everything else.

---

## 7. Follow-up

Tap **Follow-up** at the bottom of the screen to see every patient you have referred who
still needs checking, soonest first. Overdue ones are marked in red.

When you have seen a patient and the referral is done, tap **Mark done**.

---

## 8. Health information

Tap **Learn** at the bottom of the screen.

Here you will find health messages to share with patients, covering high blood pressure,
diabetes, danger signs in pregnancy, danger signs in young children, malaria, nutrition
and preventing illness.

- Choose a **language** at the top.
- **Search** for a topic, or filter by category.
- Where there is a recording, a player appears at the top of the topic. **The audio plays
  without internet.**

Use these when you screen a patient: a GREEN result is still a good moment to share the
relevant message.

> If a topic is marked as a **draft translation**, the wording has not yet been checked
> by a health educator. Use the English version if you are unsure.

---

## 9. Sending records

Tap **View sync details** on the home screen to see exactly what is waiting.

Normally you never need this screen. Use it when:

- You want to check whether a particular day's work has reached the facility.
- Your supervisor asks how many records are waiting.
- Something is marked **Needs attention**.

### If something says "Needs attention"

A few records could not be accepted by the server. **The information is still on your
phone and has not been lost.** Show this screen to your supervisor. They can see what
went wrong and tell you what to do. You can tap **Try again** at any time.

---

## 10. Things to remember

1. **Your work is saved the moment you tap Save.** Not when you get signal.
2. **Never delete the application** while records are waiting to send. If you need to
   sign out completely, the application will stop you until everything has been sent.
3. **Lock the phone when you put it down.** One tap, top right.
4. **Leave measurements blank if you did not take them.** Never guess.
5. **Charge the phone.** A flat battery does not lose records, but it does stop you
   working.
6. **Refer when you are worried**, whatever the screen says.
7. **Never send health details by text message.** The application already makes sure the
   message it prepares is safe — do not add anything to it.

---

## 11. Problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Forgot my PIN | Contact your supervisor. The phone must be set up again, so tell them if records were still waiting to send |
| It says OFFLINE all day | Normal in an area with no coverage. Keep working; records send when you reach signal |
| It has not sent for several days | Go somewhere with coverage and open the application. If it still does not send, tell your supervisor |
| A record says "Needs attention" | Show the sync details screen to your supervisor. The record is not lost |
| The application will not open | Restart the phone. If it still will not open, contact your supervisor — your records are stored safely on the phone and will still be there |
| I recorded the wrong reading | Open the patient and record a new screening with the correct measurement. Tell the facility if a referral was already sent |
| A patient wants their data removed | Tell your supervisor. Patients have the right to ask, and the facility handles the request |
